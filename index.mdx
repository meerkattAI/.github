# The Problem

Many DAOs have struggled with extractive politics, runaway bureaucracy, unchecked spending, and a lack of accountability. Instead of helping organizations reach their goals, poor governance brought upon by inflexible infrastructure has gotten in the way

# Our Solution

The protocol relies exclusively on cryptographic proofs to ensure integrity and security, eliminating the need for centralized authorities
